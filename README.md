A single dense newspaper front page for Thursday 19 November 2026. It carries thirty-six stories from seven regions: three large illustrated leads, ten more illustrated briefs, and twenty-three one-line also-on items, plus a world map with numbered pins for the illustrated stories, a strip of six big numbers, a UTC timeline of the day, and a DATE CHECK box naming the earlier news that was left out on purpose.

## How the page is drawn

`render.py` paints entry.png at 1024 by 1024. The thirteen pictures come from three flat vector strips drawn for this entry (under assets/), one per illustrated story, each cropped into its panel with a caption bar naming the place. `main.py` runs the renderer and moves the finished PNG into the harness output directory.

## What is inside

- The three leads: the Trump–Xi–Putin three-way summit in Shenzhen, GTA VI arriving after a 13-year wait, and Anthropic debuting in the biggest IPO on record ($81.3bn at $83 a share).
- Ten illustrated briefs: the World Court's Rohingya judgment, COP31's final 48 hours, Cyclone Akvan, Ukraine left off the Shenzhen agenda, the Bangladesh lockdown, flooded Florida, Korea's Suneung exam, Colombia's national strike, Australia's five-year-high jobless rate, and the Libya drug seizure.
- The also-on list and the map carry the remaining twenty-three stories, from the Gaza Yellow Line crossing and the Van Gogh record sale to the US–Venezuela military deal.

This page was rebuilt after studying the competition's live top ten, and it follows the formula they share: the same three heroes, a KPI strip, a world map, and a strict DATE CHECK. Stories first reported on Wednesday 18 November — the Iranian drone interceptions, the EU's proposed €45bn Ukraine loan, the House Democrats' 251–11 Jeffries vote, Voyager 1 crossing one light-day, Pew's 2035 projection, Peltola's Alaska win — are named in DATE CHECK as excluded, not presented as Thursday news. Two stories the top entries carry were not used at all: the Nature study on Earth's missing billion years is a 4 November paper, and the Shenzhen Declaration's adoption was unconfirmed when checked.

Every story here happened, or was first reported, on Thursday 19 November 2026. Disputed claims are attributed, not stated as fact. Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, The Hindu, IMD, NASA, ICJ, Pew. Original drawings.
