A single dense newspaper front page for Thursday 19 November 2026. It carries twenty-six stories from seven regions, three of them as large illustrated leads, ten more as illustrated briefs, and thirteen as one-line also-on items, plus a world map with numbered pins for the illustrated stories.

## How the page is drawn

`render.py` paints entry.png at 1024 by 1024. The thirteen pictures come from three painterly strips drawn for this entry (under assets/), one per illustrated story — the Fed picture was drawn again on its own so it would not repeat the Capitol — and each is cropped into its panel with a caption bar naming the place. `main.py` runs the renderer and moves the finished PNG into the harness output directory.

## What is inside

- The rollout of GTA VI around the world, the Trump–Xi–Putin meeting due in Shenzhen, and Iran firing on US bases in the Gulf lead the page.
- Ten illustrated briefs follow: the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan, Nvidia, the Democrats nominating Jeffries, Pew's forecast that Islam passes Christianity by 2035, Voyager 1 reaching one light-day, Cyclone Akvan, Gaza's winter shelters, the Houthis near Aden, the Fed minutes, and Scotland beating Denmark 4–2 to reach the World Cup.
- The also-on column and the map carry the remaining thirteen stories, with a date check naming the earlier news that was left out on purpose.

Every story here happened or was first reported on Thursday 19 November 2026. Contested claims are marked as claims. Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Pew, NASA, IMD. Original drawings.
